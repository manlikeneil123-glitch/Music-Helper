# Changelog

What changed in each version of Stave, in plain words. Written from `app/js/changelog.js`.

## 0.9.0: Day theme and Performance Grades (2026-10-30)

- Doing a Performance Grade or Trinity repertoire-only exam? Choose "Pieces only" on the Exam tab: four pieces, and no scales, arpeggios or sight-reading anywhere.
- A Day theme, and Settings: theme, still scenery (no moving background), vibration, and download all your data.
- Your streak now allows one rest day a week.
- New pages: Terms, Help and support (with UK helplines), and Accessibility.
- Text is easier to read on every screen, and the app works offline with a notice when you lose connection.

## 0.8.0: Stave (2026-10-30)

- Music Helper is now Stave, with a new glass welcome screen.
- A Skip button on setup.
- Pop-ups sit in the middle of the screen on phones.

## 0.7.0: Recording kit and real piano sound (2026-10-30)

- A real sampled piano.
- The microphone hears loud and soft playing.
- Imported scores follow D.C. and D.S. and time-signature changes, with beams, slurs and hairpins.
- A Performance Grade video recorder (the video stays on your phone).
- A recording kit for testing the microphone on your own piano. Taps and clicks no longer count as notes.

## 0.6.0: Teachers and parents (2026-10-30)

- Teachers can set work, share scores, and listen to takes students send (deleted after 20 days).
- A private parent link.
- A weekly report card to share.
- Assignment links and QR codes for teachers without accounts.

## 0.5.0: The Exam tab (2026-10-30)

- Count down to your exam, with this week's targets and a readiness band for each piece.
- A mock exam in the real order.
- Practice plans follow your grade, board and lesson day.
- Loop a few bars, or add a bar at a time.
