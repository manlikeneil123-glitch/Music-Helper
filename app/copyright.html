Copyright

For composers, publishers and anyone whose work is shared through Stave. Last updated 31 October 2026.

What people can share

Most of Stave stays on the phone: scores a student imports are never uploaded. Two things are stored on our servers and shown to other people: scores a teacher shares with the students in their own studio, and recordings a student sends to their own teacher (deleted after 20 days). Nothing is public.

Reporting something

If you believe something shared through Stave breaks your copyright, send us:

  • the work you own, and how you're connected to it (owner or acting for the owner);
  • enough detail for us to find what was shared, such as the piece's title and the teacher's username if you know it;
  • your name and a way to reach you;
  • a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't allowed, and that what you've told us is accurate (and, under US law, that you're the owner or authorised to act for them, under penalty of perjury);
  • your signature (typing your full name is enough).

We remove what you report, usually within 2 working days, and tell the person who shared it. Accounts that repeatedly share things they have no right to are closed.

Who to send it to

Our designated copyright agent: Stave copyright agent, contacted through our GitHub page. Open an issue titled "Copyright notice" with no personal details, and we'll reply with a private address for the full notice. (Our US Copyright Office registration is being completed; this page will list it.)

If your content was removed by mistake

Tell us the same way, with what was removed, why you believe it was a mistake, and your name and a way to reach you. Unless the person who reported it tells us they're going to court, we can put it back after 10 working days.
