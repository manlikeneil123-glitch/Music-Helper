Help and support

Quick answers, how to reach us, and who to talk to if things are hard.

If you need to talk to someone

If you're in danger right now, call 999.

  • Childline (under 19, free, confidential): call 0800 1111 or chat at childline.org.uk
  • Shout (24/7 text support): text SHOUT to 85258
  • Samaritans (any age, 24/7): call 116 123 or visit samaritans.org
  • NHS 111: for health worries, including pain from playing, call 111 or visit 111.nhs.uk
  • NSPCC helpline (adults worried about a child): 0808 800 5000

Exam nerves are normal. If worry about an exam is taking over, talk to your teacher, a parent or carer, or Childline. A grade can always be taken later.

Quick answers

  • How do I get started? Follow the Getting started list on Today, or take the 2-minute tour: tap your avatar, then Settings, then Show me around.
  • The microphone says I played wrong notes but I didn't. Set up the microphone first (Perform, then tap the line above Start), with the phone on the music stand and the room quiet. If it keeps happening, a MIDI keyboard gives exact results.
  • Where are the songs? Stave uses your own pieces, so you practise what you're actually learning. See Getting your piece into Stave.
  • I'm doing a Performance Grade. On the Exam tab, choose Pieces only. You get four pieces, no scales or sight-reading, and the filming checklist.
  • I forgot my password. Ask your teacher to set a temporary one (Account page, Students). Without a teacher, contact us.
  • I changed phone. Sign in on the new one and your progress comes back. Without an account, progress stays on the old device: use Settings, then Download my data first.
  • How do I install it? iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the menu, then Install app.

Getting your piece into Stave

Stave checks your playing against the real score, so it needs the piece as a MusicXML file (it ends in .musicxml, .mxl or .xml). Stave doesn't include exam pieces: they're copyrighted.

  1. From your teacher: if they use Stave, scores they share appear in Perform on their own. Otherwise ask them to send you the MusicXML file.
  2. From MuseScore.com: many pieces are there. Open the piece, choose Download, then MusicXML. Check it matches your edition.
  3. Type it in yourself: the free MuseScore Studio app lets you enter the notes, then File, Export, MusicXML. Good practice for reading, too.
  4. Take a photo of the music in Perform (or + Photo): photograph the page, send it with Stave's message to an AI chat app you're allowed to use (such as ChatGPT or Gemini), and paste the reply back. Stave shows what it read next to your photo so you can check every bar.
  5. Other scanning apps (PlayScore, Soundslice, or MuseScore's import) can work, but always check the notes afterwards: one wrong note in the file means Stave marks your right note as wrong.

Then, in Perform, tap Add a file (or + File) and choose it. On a phone, save the file to Files (iPhone) or Downloads (Android) first.

How Stave marks

After every play-through Stave gives an estimated mark using your exam board's own headings. It is not an exam mark: only an examiner can give one. Stave marks a little strictly, so the real exam shouldn't surprise you.

  • ABRSM, out of 30: Pitch, Time, Tone, Shape and Performance. Pass 20, Merit 24, Distinction 27.
  • Trinity, out of 22: Accuracy and fluency (7), Technical facility (7), Communication and interpretation (8). Repertoire-only pieces are out of 25.

What Stave listens for:

  • Pitch: how many notes were right, and how many were wrong, missed or extra.
  • Time: rhythm, how steady your beat was, and your speed compared with the marked speed. Playing much slower than marked, or rushing, lowers it.
  • Tone: how far apart your loud and soft playing were.
  • Shape: whether the changes of dynamic in the music (p, mf, f, crescendos) came through, and the staccato and legato.
  • Performance: keeping going after a slip, not stopping, and reaching the end.

The strict rules: Distinction needs every heading at Merit or better, and the overall mark can be at most one band above your weakest heading. If you play only part of the piece, Stave works out which bars and marks just those.

With a microphone, Tone and Shape are rough: a phone hears loudness, not touch, and on busy music it can't tell the hands apart. When it can't hear them clearly they count as Pass level, so Distinction is hard to reach on a phone. A digital piano with a cable gives Stave the exact loudness, pedal and note lengths.

For teachers

  • Set work without an account: in Perform, open More, then "Practise one tricky part over and over", choose the bars and tap "Teachers: send this as an assignment link". The student opens the link or scans the QR code, and the work goes to the top of their Today plan.
  • With a teacher account: your studio code lets students join. You can share scores, set work, hear recordings students send you (deleted after 20 days) and approve under-13 accounts.
  • Students need the same piece in Stave as you: share the MusicXML file, or share the score from your studio.

For parents, carers and teachers

  • Stave has no adverts, no in-app purchases and no chat between students.
  • Nothing is shared by default. A student chooses to join a teacher's studio (with the teacher's code) and can leave at any time.
  • Parent view: parents and carers can follow progress through a private link. The student makes it once they have an account: Settings and account, then Share with a parent. It shows a summary, never recordings or messages, and the student can turn it off.
  • Teachers see each student's practice summary and the recordings a student chooses to send (deleted after 20 days).
  • Under 13s should have an account set up by a parent or carer. The app works fully without an account.
