Help and support

Quick answers, how to reach us, and who to talk to if things are hard.

If you need to talk to someone

If you're in danger right now, call 999.

  • Childline (under 19, free, confidential): call 0800 1111 or chat at childline.org.uk
  • Shout (24/7 text support): text SHOUT to 85258
  • Samaritans (any age, 24/7): call 116 123 or visit samaritans.org
  • NHS 111: for health worries, including pain from playing, call 111 or visit 111.nhs.uk
  • NSPCC helpline (adults worried about a child): 0808 800 5000

Exam nerves are normal. If worry about an exam is taking over, talk to your teacher, a parent or carer, or Childline. A grade can always be taken later.

Quick answers

  • How do I get started? Follow the Getting started list on Today, or take the 2-minute tour: tap your avatar, then Settings, then Show me around.
  • The microphone says I played wrong notes but I didn't. Set up the microphone first (Perform, then tap the line above Start), with the phone on the music stand and the room quiet. If it keeps happening, a MIDI keyboard gives exact results.
  • Where are the songs? Stave uses your own pieces, so you practise what you're actually learning. See Getting your piece into Stave.
  • I'm doing a Performance Grade. On the Exam tab, choose Pieces only. You get four pieces, no scales or sight-reading, and the filming checklist.
  • I forgot my password. Ask your teacher to set a temporary one (Account page, Students). Without a teacher, contact us.
  • I changed phone. Sign in on the new one and your progress comes back. Without an account, progress stays on the old device: use Settings, then Download my data first.
  • How do I install it? iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the menu, then Install app.

Getting your piece into Stave

Stave checks your playing against the real score, so it needs the piece as a MusicXML file (it ends in .musicxml, .mxl or .xml). Stave doesn't include exam pieces: they're copyrighted.

  1. From your teacher: if they use Stave, scores they share appear in Perform on their own. Otherwise ask them to send you the MusicXML file.
  2. From MuseScore.com: many pieces are there. Open the piece, choose Download, then MusicXML. Check it matches your edition.
  3. Type it in yourself: the free MuseScore Studio app lets you enter the notes, then File, Export, MusicXML. Good practice for reading, too.
  4. Scanning a printed page (PlayScore, Soundslice, or MuseScore's import) can work, but always check the notes afterwards: one wrong note in the file means Stave marks your right note as wrong.

Then, in Perform, tap Add a piece and choose the file. On a phone, save the file to Files (iPhone) or Downloads (Android) first.

For parents, carers and teachers

  • Stave has no adverts, no in-app purchases and no chat between students.
  • Nothing is shared by default. A student chooses to join a teacher's studio (with the teacher's code) and can leave at any time.
  • Parents and carers can follow progress through a private link the student makes from their Account page. It shows a summary, never recordings or messages.
  • Teachers see each student's practice summary and the recordings a student chooses to send (deleted after 20 days).
  • Under 13s should have an account set up by a parent or carer. The app works fully without an account.
