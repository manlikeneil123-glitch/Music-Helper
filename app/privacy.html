Privacy

For students, parents and teachers. Short version: no adverts, no tracking, no selling data, and as little as we can manage. Last updated 30 October 2026.

Without an account

Everything stays on your device: your practice log, check results, flashcard progress and settings. Nothing is sent anywhere. Recordings the microphone makes for checking are analysed on the device and thrown away.

With an account

Accounts are a username and password. We don't ask for your name, email, age, school or phone number.

We never store: recordings (unless you choose to send one to your teacher; see below), your location, contacts, device details or anything for advertising.

Sending a recording to your teacher

Only when you press send. It goes only to your own teacher, and is deleted automatically after 20 days, or sooner if you or your teacher delete it.

Work from your teacher

If you're in a teacher's studio, the app downloads the work they set you and any scores they share with their students. Shared scores are only ever visible to that teacher's students. Assignment links a teacher sends you contain only the piece, bars, tempo, due date and their note, and are kept on your device.

Parent link

You can make a private link for a parent or carer. It shows a summary only (practice, streak, recent check scores, exam readiness, the bars you're working on), never recordings or messages. Making a new link turns the old one off, and you can turn it off completely from your Account page.

Performance videos

Videos you record for a Performance Grade stay on your phone. They are never uploaded by us.

Your teacher

You choose whether to join a teacher's studio, using a code they give you. You can leave at any time from your Account page, and they stop seeing your progress straight away. A teacher can remove you, and can set a temporary password if you forget yours.

Deleting everything

Account → Delete account removes your account and everything stored with it, permanently. Signing out removes your progress from that device (it stays in your account).

Where it's kept

Accounts use Supabase, with data stored in the UK/EU. Each account can only read its own data (and a teacher their students' summaries); this is enforced by the database, not just the app.

Under 13

If you're under 13, a parent or carer should set up the account. Without an account, the app needs no permission at all.

Questions

Ask your teacher, or contact the app's maker through the details on the App Store listing.
