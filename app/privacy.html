Privacy

For students, parents, carers and teachers. Last updated 30 October 2026.

Our promises

  • No adverts.
  • We never sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • No tracking or analytics: we don't record what you tap or how long you stay.
  • Nothing is shared with anyone unless you choose to (joining a teacher's studio, sending a recording, making a parent link).

1. Who we are

Stave is a free piano practice app made by an independent developer in the UK. It is not connected to ABRSM or Trinity College London. To contact us, open an issue on our GitHub page. Issues there are public, so don't post anything personal: just say you'd like a private reply, and we'll give you a way to reach us privately.

2. What we collect

Without an account, nothing you do leaves your device. Your practice log, check results, flashcard progress, exam plan and settings are kept in your browser's storage.

With an account (a username and password; we never ask for your name, email, age, school or phone number):

Technical data: the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers see your IP address and browser type when the app loads (GitHub's privacy statement). We don't receive or keep these logs.

3. Health and body data

None. Stave doesn't ask about or store anything about your health or body.

4. Sound, recordings, videos and files

  • Microphone: when you check a piece, the sound is analysed on your device and thrown away. It is never uploaded.
  • Sending a recording to your teacher: only when you press send. It goes only to your own teacher, and is deleted automatically after 20 days, or sooner if you or your teacher delete it.
  • Performance Grade videos stay on your phone. We never upload them; you submit them to the exam board yourself.
  • Imported scores (MusicXML files) are kept on your device, and in your account if you have one.
  • The recording kit (in Perform, for testing the microphone) saves a .zip on your device. It only goes anywhere if you send it yourself.

5. Why we're allowed to use it

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR): your account data, to provide the service you signed up for (contract); recordings you send and parent links, because you chose to (consent, which you can take back by deleting them); and keeping accounts secure (legitimate interests).

6. Who can see what

Private by default. Your teacher sees a summary only if you join their studio with their code, and stops seeing it the moment you leave (Account page). A parent or carer sees a summary only through a private link you make; making a new link turns the old one off, and you can turn it off completely. Teachers can remove a student and set a temporary password if one is forgotten. The database enforces these rules itself, not just the app.

7. Who helps us run it

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • Supabase: stores accounts and synced progress, in the UK/EU. Only used if you make an account.

Fonts, piano sounds and images are served from our own site, so no other company sees your visit.

8. AI

Stave uses no AI services. Checking your playing happens on your device.

9. Connected devices

If you plug in a MIDI keyboard, the browser passes its notes to the app on your device. Nothing about the keyboard is sent anywhere. You can withdraw the browser's MIDI or microphone permission at any time in its settings.

10. What stays only on your device

Microphone calibration, which tab you were on, a running timer, a mock exam in progress, your theme and motion settings, and everything else if you don't have an account.

11. How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account. Deleting removes it straight away; we keep no backup copies of deleted accounts.
  • Recordings sent to a teacher: 20 days at most.
  • Parent links: until you turn them off or make a new one.
  • Data on your device: until you clear it, sign out, or uninstall the app.

12. Your rights

  • See and download everything: Settings, then Download my data (a JSON file).
  • Correct it: change your setup, exam plan and pieces in the app.
  • Delete it: Account, then Delete account (permanent). Without an account, clear the site's data in your browser.
  • Object or complain: contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

13. Young people

Most people using Stave are under 18, so we follow the UK Age Appropriate Design Code: the most private settings are the default, nothing is shared unless you choose, there are no adverts or nudges to share more, and no tricks to keep you in the app. You need to be 13 or over to make an account yourself; if you're younger, a parent or carer should set it up. Without an account, the app needs no permission at all. Parents, carers and teachers: see Help and support.

14. Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed by Supabase and never seen by us. Each account can read only its own data (and a teacher their own students' summaries), enforced by row-level security in the database.

15. Changes to this policy

When something changes we update the date at the top and mention it in the app's What's new note. We won't start using your data in a new way without asking.
