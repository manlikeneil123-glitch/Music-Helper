Agreement

By using Stave you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use it.

Who can use it

Anyone can use Stave without an account. To make an account yourself you need to be 13 or over; if you're younger, a parent or carer should set it up for you.

Health and safety

Stave isn't medical advice. Take breaks, keep your hands and shoulders relaxed, and stop if playing hurts. If pain in your hands, wrists, arms or back keeps coming back, tell your teacher and see a doctor (or call NHS 111). Keep headphone volume moderate.

Exams

Stave isn't connected to ABRSM or Trinity College London. Readiness bands estimate pitch and time from your own runs; they are not exam marks, and only an examiner can mark your exam. Always check the board's current syllabus and rules, especially before submitting a Performance Grade or digital exam video.

Using it fairly

  • Be kind. Teachers' comments and anything you send are for practice, not for upsetting anyone.
  • Teachers: only add your own students, use reset passwords only to help a student back in, and never sign in as a student for any other reason.
  • Don't share other people's recordings, or try to get into accounts or data that aren't yours.

Your content

What you record, import and write stays yours. You let us store it and show it to the people you choose (your teacher, a parent link), only so the app can work. Only import or share scores you're allowed to use; teachers sharing scores should share them only with their own students.

The service

Stave is free while we're testing and is provided as it is: we work hard to make it right, but we can't promise it is always available or that every check is correct. Features may change. Microphone checking can mishear, especially on unusual pianos or in noisy rooms.

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from the Account page. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms, for example by harassing other users.
